About

Hi, I’m Noel Covarrubias. I go by Noel Codes online.

I’m a software engineer with about four years of experience. I've worked at Microsoft, Clover, and JPMorgan. Most of my work has been on backend systems: APIs, microservices, infrastructure, and deployment pipelines. I’ve also worked on helping my team adopt AI tools.

Outside of work, I spend a lot of time reading, writing, learning, and working out.

I read for about an hour every day, usually about technology, investing, health, self-help or business. I started writing last year to work through difficult problems at work, and kept going because it helps me think more clearly.

Calisthenics is a big part of my life. I used to focus on weightlifting and powerlifting, but switched about two years ago. These days, I’m practicing handstands and muscle-ups, working toward a V-sit, and training for a full front lever and planche.

If you’re into calisthenics, have a book you love recommending, or enjoy talking through something you’re learning, let's connect.
